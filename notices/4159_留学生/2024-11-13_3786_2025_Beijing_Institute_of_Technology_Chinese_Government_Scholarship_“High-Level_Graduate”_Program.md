2025 Beijing Institute of Technology Chinese Government Scholarship “High-Level Graduate” Program

1. Program Description

Chinese Government Scholarship “High-Level Graduate” Program is a full scholarship program by China's Ministry of Education to some of China's top universities for the direct selection and admission of outstanding international students to study in China.

Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT) is one of the host institutions of the program and is responsible for recruiting high-level international students with excellent academic background, professional abilities, and future development potential to study for master's and doctoral degrees.

3. Eligibility

To be eligible, an applicant must:

1. be non-Chinese citizens and in good health both mentally and physically, and will observe the Chinese laws and the regulations, and the rules and regulations of Beijing Institute of Technology;

2. be an academically excellent holder of a bachelor’s degree or its equivalent under the age of 35 when applying for a master’s program;

3. be an academically excellent holder of a master’s degree or its equivalent under the age of 40 when applying for a Ph.D. program;

4. hold an HSK certificate (minimum level 5 with a score of 180 or above) for Chinese-taught programs; for English-taught programs, a minimum score of 85 in TOEFL, an overall band of6.0 in IELTS, or a score of 110 in the Duolingo English Test is required. Alternatively, other language certificates recognized by BlT may be provided (native English speakers are exempt);

5. have at least 1-year related working or internship experience for MBA program.

4. Coverage of the Scholarship

1. Tuition fees;

2. Free university dormitory or accommodation subsidy;

3. Stipend:

Master’s students: CNY 3,000 /month,

Doctoral students: CNY 3,500/month;

4. Comprehensive medical insurance: CNY 800/person/year.

6. Application Procedure

(1) Register at the Chinese Government Scholarship Information System (https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn) and upload required documents. (BlT's Agency No. is 10007; Type: B).

(2) Register at the
BIT International Student Service System (
http://apply.isc.bit.edu.cn
)
and upload required documents.

7. Application Materials

(1) CSC application form (For CSC applicants);

(2) The Photocopy of Passport (Home Page and China Visa Page if applicants have). If the validity of the applicant’s current passport does not meet the requirement (meaning the passport expires before September 2025), please apply for a new passport before submitting the application;

(3) The Certified/Notarized Copy of highest Degree's Diploma/ Certificate of Schooling (in Chinese or English);

(4) The Certified/Notarized Copy of Academic Transcripts (undergraduate level and above, if any, in Chinese or English);

(5) Curriculum Vitae;

(6) Personal Statement & Study Plan;

(7) Two Recommendation Letters by professors or associate professors;

(8) Recommendation Form for International Student by BIT Supervisors;

(9) Physical Examination Record for Foreigner(with the blood test report);

(10) Proof of Language Proficiency;

(11) Non-criminal Record;

(12) Financial Guarantee Letter;

(13) Other Supporting Documents (eg, papers/patent/certificates, etc.).
