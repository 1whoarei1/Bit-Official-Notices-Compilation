## 1+1 Mode

Study in China in year 1; writing thesis remotely in year 2

## Leadership Capacity Building

Discover leadership style, learn strategy, become better communicator, and advance technical capabilities, via a series of workshops, seminars, and participating in international events.

## Our Aim is to Cultivate and Strengthen the Capabilities of Global Governance

1. Fostering an international, culturally sensitive mindset,

2. Advancing managerial and leadership analytical and decision-making skills,

3. Enhancing understanding of science, technology and engineering,

4. Deepening the understanding of diplomatic relations in the political arena, and

5. Sharpening socially responsible global leadership abilities.

## Who Should Take our Program?

\- Officials & Staff from governments, United Nations and international non-governmental organizations (INGO),

• Middle and senior managers from public and private institutions,

\- Administrators from academic and research institutions, and

\- Individuals with relevant experience or education.
