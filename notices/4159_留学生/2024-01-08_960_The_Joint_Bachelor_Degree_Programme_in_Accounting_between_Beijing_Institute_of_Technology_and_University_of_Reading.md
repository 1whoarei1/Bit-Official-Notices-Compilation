The Joint Bachelor Degree Programme in Accounting between Beijing Institute of Technology and University of Reading

北京理工大学与英国瑞丁大学合作举办会计学专业本科教育项目

会计学（中外合作办学）

The Joint Programme approved by the Ministry of Education of China since 2013 allows applicants to study at Beijing Institute of Technology for four years and grants the qualified candidates Bachelor of Management in Accounting by Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT), China and Bachelor of Arts in Accounting by University of Reading (UoR), the United Kingdom, respectively.

About University of Reading and Henley Business School

The University of Reading has been at the forefront of UK higher education for nearly a century. In 2016, it celebrated the 90th anniversary of its Royal Charter. Over the decades, the University has become innovators and pioneers, pushing academic boundaries and leading social change. The Joint Programme is run by Henley Business School, University of Reading.

Henley Business School at the University of Reading, one of the first business schools in the UK (1945), is known for cultivating outstanding business talents and leaders. With campuses, offices and partnerships around the world, over 7,000 students from more than 100 countries and over 87,000 alumni from 160 countries, the School is truly international. It is Europe's leading business school, a triple accredited (AMBA, EQUIS, AACSB) business school in 2002. In the Financial Times (Financial Times 2022) European Business School Rankings, Henley Business School is ranked 7th in the UK. In terms of individual subject rankings, both the MSc in Finance and the MSc in Management are in the top 10 in the UK, and the real estate major has ranked first for three consecutive years. Its ICMA Centre (International Capital Market Association Center) has also been recognized worldwide for its excellent teaching and research level. "Dealing Rooms is the largest training base in Europe that provides education for international securities and investment professionals.

I. Qualification

1. High school graduate or above who is qualified to apply for university; non-Chinese citizens with valid foreign passport and in good health; observe Chinese laws and regulations of Beijing Institute of Technology.

2. Aged between 18 and 25; Applicants under 18 should provide certificate of guardian who has Beijing residency.

3. Applicants must comply with the regulations (JW〔2020〕No. 12) issued by Ministry of Education of People's Republic of China.

4. For applicants from English-speaking and non-English speaking countries or regions:

·Good command of mathematics with sound logical analysis capability, a valid report of GMAT no lower than 650 or GRE no lower than 156. Successful completion of a Senior High School including grades of graduation in Mathematics at no lower than GPA of 3.2 or higher on a 4.0 scale. (For applicants not yet hold evidence proving their high school level proficiencies in mathematics, a Placement Test conducted by BIT Office of International Students will be available twice a year for them to take at no lower than 80% on a 100 percent scale.)

·English Language: IELTS 6.5 with no component lower than 5.5; or TOEFL 96, with reading and writing no lower than 24/30, oral speaking and listening no lower than 22/30.

·Chinese Language: a minimum score of 180 in HSK Test (Level 5).
