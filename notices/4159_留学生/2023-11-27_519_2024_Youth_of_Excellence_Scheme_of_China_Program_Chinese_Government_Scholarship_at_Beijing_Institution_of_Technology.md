2024 Youth of Excellence Scheme of China Program Chinese Government Scholarship at Beijing Institution of Technology

The Youth of Excellence Scheme of China Program Chinese Government Scholarship was established in 2014, aiming to train talents in global governance with an international mindset, excellent overall quality, outstanding leadership skills, and a multi-cultural background. The English-taught Master's programs in Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science and Technology, and International Organizations and Global Governance in Beijing Institution of Technology have been successfully selected for the Youth of Excellence Scheme of China Program Chinese Government Scholarship in the 2024-2025 academic year.

2. Training mode and duration:

The program adopts the "1+1" mode. In the first academic year, applicants complete the full-time course study, research work, and dissertation preparation at Beijing Institute of Technology. In the second academic year, applicants return to their home countries to complete the dissertation in the light of their own professional work. They come to China to complete the dissertation defense as required. Those who pass will be awarded the degree.

3. Amount of funding:
Full scholarship

II.
How to Apply

1.
Eligibility:

Applicants should meet the following requirements:

(1) be non-Chinese citizens under the age of 45 and in good mental and physical health;

(2) be an academically excellent holder of a bachelor’s degree with 3-year related working experience (at minimum) ; those with professional study or working background related to the applied program are preferred;

(3) Applicants must be one of the following four categories:

① Public officials at the division level (or corresponding level) or above in the government department of the host country;

② Senior managers of institutions, enterprises, or other units;

③ Administrators of universities and research institutions;

④ Persons with relevant experience(s) in international organizations (full-time or internship).

III.
Review and Admission

1. The China Scholarship Council will conduct a preliminary examination of the application materials and organize experts to evaluate the applications, and the final admission result will be determined after the expert evaluation.

2. Each scholarship recipient can only receive funding from one Chinese Government Scholarship program.

3. If the scholarship recipient cannot come to study in China according to the admission deadline, the scholarship status will not be retained.
