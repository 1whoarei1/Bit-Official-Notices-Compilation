项目概述：
The TU Berlin Winter School takes place from 06 January to 30 January and offers intensive short courses of 2 to 4 weeks in duration. Choose from a wide rage of courses in many academic fields and study with your international peers.

项目组织机构：柏林工业大学

二、部分课程信息

（完整课程信息见柏林工业大学官方网站：https://www.tu.berlin/en/international/summer-school/winter-school）

①《Sustainable Buildings: Principles and Modelling》

Learn how to design a building in a sustainable way while working on small-scale projects in Berlin.

②
《
Industry 5.0
》

Explore how AI can be ethically and effectively integrated into human-centered industries. Learn in a globally oriented course how Industry 5.0 is being developed and applied across different cultural, policy, and business contexts

③《Gaming for Virtual and Augmented Reality》

Learn the fundamentals of MR, UX design, and programming for extended reality with Unity. Register now and get hands-on experience in creating your own extended reality app.

④《Innovation and Entrepreneurship》

Create your own new product from idea through business concept development, testing and pitching by getting introduced to human-centered design and innovation eco-systems.
